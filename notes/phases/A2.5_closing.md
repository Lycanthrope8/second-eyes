# A2.5: closing note (10 October 2026)

A2.5 integrates the base model on the Quest (D98, D99):

1. a typed command and a versioned scene snapshot;
2. the prompt;
3. local offered-letter scoring;
4. the object ID or ASK.

This note gathers each delivery's status and the four statuses that must stay separate. Accepting A2.5 is the project
lead's and ChatGPT's decision.

## The four statuses, kept separate

1. **The functional pipeline:** demonstrated on the headset, operationally on path U (uncached, prior KV cleared, the
   complete prompt evaluated). Every outcome records its execution path. Failures keep their reasons, and an ASK
   records its basis.
2. **Uncached numerical acceptance against float32:** not established. U against float32 fails D101 in delivery 1.
3. **Cached numerical acceptance under D56/D101:** not established. R/U and P/U fail D101. Prefix reuse is refused
   operationally until acceptance, or an explicit amendment by the project lead.
   - The bounded batch-arithmetic diagnostic (r021) supports, on its four cases, the hypothesis that batch-size kernel
     dispatch at the pinned commit explains the cached-path differences. It is not proof, and it is not acceptance.
4. **Latency and resources:** measured, against no threshold.
   - **Latency:** the app-observed latency of an unqueued command is about 11.5 s for 3 objects, 17.2 s for 6 and
     28.1 s for 10. Native evaluation takes medians of 11.50, 17.09 and 27.98 s.
   - **Memory:** resident from about 1.507 GiB (1.618 GB) after the load, to at most 1.571 GiB (1.687 GB).
   - **Not captured:** frames and heat.

Delivery 3's diagnostic check (r016) shows what is at stake in status 3. With the cache, a command took a median 0.25 s
instead of 17.1 s, and all 32 choices were the same. That is not acceptance.
