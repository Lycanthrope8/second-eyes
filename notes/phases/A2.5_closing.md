# A2.5: closing note (10 October 2026)

A2.5 integrates the base model on the Quest (D98, D99):

1. a typed command and a versioned scene snapshot;
2. the prompt;
3. local offered-letter scoring;
4. the object ID or ASK.

This note gathers each delivery's status and the four statuses that must stay separate. Accepting A2.5 is the project
lead's and ChatGPT's decision.

## The deliveries

| Delivery | What | Status | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | frozen-request replay on the headset and the desktop | **acceptance failed (D101)**. Repeatability shown (r013); checkpoint and export correspondence and compatible configuration established (provenance) | `A2.5_d1_replay.md` |
| 2 | the headset's prompt builder: goldens, the C# core, self-checks | **closed** for its approved scope (r014, r015) | `A2.5_d2_prompting.md` |

| 5 | the measurement | **measured** (r017, r019, r020; r018 an incomplete first attempt) | `A2.5_d5_measurement.md` |

**Delivery 4's behaviours, each seen on the headset:**

- the inbox delivered by temporary name and rename, with an acknowledgement and a result under each ID;
- duplicates ignored;
- stale scene bindings refused;
- a written command;
- cancel while queued, and during evaluation;
- stale results refused after a scene switch;
- End session answering every waiting request.

## The four statuses, kept separate

1. **The functional pipeline:** established on the headset, operationally on path U (uncached, prior KV cleared, the
   complete prompt evaluated). Every outcome records its execution path. Failures keep their reasons, and an ASK
   records its basis.
2. **Uncached numerical acceptance against float32:** not established. U against float32 fails D101 in delivery 1. The
   bounded batch-arithmetic diagnostic (`A2.5_d1_replay.md`, section 11) is proposed and awaiting approval.
3. **Cached numerical acceptance under D56/D101:** not established. R/U and P/U fail D101. Prefix reuse is refused
   operationally until acceptance, or an explicit amendment by the project lead.
4. **Latency and resources:** measured, against no threshold. The app-observed latency of an unqueued command is about
   11.5 s for 3 objects, 17.2 s for 6 and 28.1 s for 10. Almost all of it is the uncached evaluation, about 110 to 136
   tokens a second. Memory is about 1.58 to 1.65 GB resident with the model loaded. Frames and heat were not captured.

Delivery 3's diagnostic check (r016) shows what is at stake in status 3. With the cache, a command took a median
0.25 s instead of 17.1 s, and all 32 choices were the same. That is not acceptance.
