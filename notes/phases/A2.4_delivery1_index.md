## Evidence: what was tested where

**Sandbox:** Linux, one CPU core.
- Python 3.12.3: torch 2.11.0+cu130 on the CPU (the RTX PC uses the cu128 build of 2.11.0), transformers 4.57.6,
  tokenizers 0.22.2, PEFT 0.21.0, accelerate 1.15.0, jsonschema 4.26.0.
- Python 3.11.15: the same without torch (disk).

**Stand-in models** are random-weight Qwen2 models with the real 151,936-token vocabulary and the pinned tokenizer
files, labelled as stand-ins. Fits used 2 layers; the evaluation path used 24 layers shaped like the 0.5B. They
establish integration only.

**The project lead's real-hardware runs (Stage A) are the acceptance.** Nothing below replaces them.

**Revision 3 (2026-10-12)** adds explicit checks, in the preparation and training suites, that every drawn scan was prepared. Any failure is printed with its error and lock retries. The real preparation reports failed scans in its summary and in `pilot-spec.md`. The preparation and training suites were rerun under the cp1252 console.
