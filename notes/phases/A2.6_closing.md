## Next: A2.4 (not started)

ChatGPT's instruction for A2.4 is recorded here for when the project lead starts it: **one consolidated
training-preparation and pilot delivery.**

- **The designs:** LoRA on the pinned 0.5B, comparing direct selection with query parsing feeding the shared resolver.
  Both formats are kept, and the 7B is an empirical reference.
- **The data:** training examples from training environments; development guides selection; legacy is separate; the
  closed partitions stay closed.
- **The pilot:** about 1,000 unique training parent commands, counted separately from format and view variants. The
  budget of about 20,000 examples stays conditional on development findings.
- **Labels:**
  - training-label compatibility is established before training;
  - a unique source target alone does not prove that the visible evidence supports it;
  - parsing supervision is verified against the query contract, disagreements are preserved, and the rule parser's
    output is not automatically ground truth.
- **Inputs and outputs:** reference annotations stay out of model inputs. Direct selection keeps D104. Parsing uses
  constrained, validated output, and malformed output is a visible failure.
- **The A2.6 development benchmark** (specification `9fa98001…`, requests `dc725cfe…`) stays the before-and-after
  comparison. Any further cohort is versioned and reported separately.
- **The pilot specification** is to be concrete, and reviewed for label validity before any long execution.
- **ChatGPT's proposal** (not a decision): four initial configurations (two designs × two formats) on a common set of
  parents with trustworthy labels for both designs. How the two inventory views enter training, and their costs, is to
  be stated.
