an end-to-end test of `bundle` and `compare` on A2.4's fixture. Its records add three rows to `notes/failures.md`, a
dated correction to the Delivery 1 runbook and a timing note to the docs. The export from Delivery 1's Part 2 stays.

Save this file and `a27-fix1.zip` in the **RTX PC's** Downloads. One block, on the RTX PC:
1. apply, test and commit fix 1, which takes about 10 minutes;
2. build the bundle and the transfer archive in `a27-20261013-200925`, beside the export. That takes 45 minutes or
   more, mostly verifying A2.6's bundles, and prints nothing until it ends.
