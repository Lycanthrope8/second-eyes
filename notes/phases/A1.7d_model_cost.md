## 6. What failed and why

- **Meta's runner fails Gate A's frame rate at every setting**: 26–66 fps on average, 42–85% of seconds below 71 fps.
  With many steps per frame it schedules a whole pass at once and then waits on the main thread for its result, so
  frames stop until the arithmetic finishes; with few steps per frame the waiting ends but every token takes twice as
  long. At 50 steps a token takes as long as at 150, with three times the frame rate while answering.
- **Latency**: 6 s to the first token and 24 s for the fixed answer, against Gate A's 3 s. A token takes 1.2 s for
  about 1 GFLOP of arithmetic, so most of the time goes elsewhere; the PC's 5.5-fold slowdown with 16-bit weights
  (r014) points at converting the weights on every pass.
- **Tool faults, fixed**: `check_headset.py` called an answer stopped before its first token a mismatch (a false FAIL
  on run 6); the table's purposes kept a YAML comment; frame rates averaged only the rows, hiding the frozen seconds.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

The sweep runs lasted 5 minutes, not 3 (D50); the runs were renumbered 1–6.

## 8. Open issues handed to the next phase

- **A1.8** writes our own runner. In order of expected effect: never wait on the main thread (read results
  asynchronously); score only the last position of a prompt; reuse the cached system prompt and scene, so a command
  computes only its own tokens; score the candidate IDs directly instead of writing 15 tokens of JSON; stop converting
  weights on every pass, if memory allows.
- **Memory headroom**: 350–600 MB free with the model loaded, and the idle model paged out within minutes; speech
  (A1.9) and the detector (A1.10) must fit in this. O14 still stands.
- **Until A1.8's runner exists**, 50 steps per frame is the better setting for Meta's runner.
