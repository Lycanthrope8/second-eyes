**What the new checks establish:**
- known `center_m` and `size_m` values survive at full precision with their state, unknown values stay unknown with no
  value, a missing field is absent, and only `audit.md`'s display rounds;
- `audit-export` keeps the same examples in the same order, with their cells, labels and checks;
- every visible object's `center_m` and `size_m` equal its original derived scene's fields exactly;
- each view links by SHA-256 to its original derived scene and command, byte for byte, matching the bundle's index;
  each parent links to its source annotation entries; and every record is stored once;
- the manifest records the preparation's identity, the superseded audit's hashes, and the nine unchanged files; the
  preparation is untouched;
- a derived scene that differs from its bundle's index is refused, and so is an existing output folder.

**Not established here:** the export of the RTX PC's real audit. Part 2 makes it.

**A design note.** The bundle index is keyed by derived command, because a scan's one full-inventory scene is shared
by many commands. The first version keyed it by scene and refused 14 full-inventory views in the fixture; it was
corrected before delivery.
