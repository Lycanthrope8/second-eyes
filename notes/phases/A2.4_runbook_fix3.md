# A2.4 fix 3: the runbook (the audit's geometry, re-exported for the review)

**Package:** `a24-fix3.zip`, SHA-256 `8b9005d699a29a8337f1a09d82f91c092ee4ff0e744487b042aa9b4df41050c5`. It goes on top of `ed10be1` (A2.4 fix 2, as pushed).

**Save both files in the laptop's Downloads folder:** the zip, and this runbook as `A2.4_runbook_fix3.md`. Part 1
copies the runbook into `notes\phases\` and commits it.

**What fix 3 does.** It follows ChatGPT's Stage A review (D112):
- `_visible()` reads the scene contract's geometry fields, `center_m`, `size_m` and `rotation_xyzw`, exactly as
  recorded: state kept, full precision, rounded for display only. The first audit read `center`, `centre` and `size`,
  so all 1,554 object entries showed null geometry.
- `audit-export` re-exports the same 143 audit examples from the saved preparation and work records into a new folder.
  It adds the original derived scene and command records and their source annotation entries, deduplicated and linked
  by SHA-256. Its manifest records the preparation's identity, the superseded audit's hashes, and that the
  specification, cohorts, labels and six training-data files are unchanged. It refuses if a derived record differs from
  its bundle's index, or a letter from the original audit's.
- A focused regression check: known `center_m` and `size_m` values survive at full precision, and unknown values stay
  unknown.
- The records: D112 (the review's outcome), one failure row, three limitations, and the plan note.

Nothing else is repeated: the preparation, preflight, grammar check and smoke stand.
