## 1. What was built

- **Scene format v2.**
  - `schemas/scene.v2.json` is v1 with one change: a closed `dataset_identity` conversion, whose identity matrix is a
    schema constant.
  - `grounding/contract/` registers it and adds `E_FRAME_SOURCE` (the conversion source isn't a dataset source in
    `sources`, including an unresolved one) and `E_FRAME_CONVERSION` (the source frame isn't the scene frame). Both
    are checked for v2 only; v1 is unchanged.
  - The contract fixtures gain a valid v2 scene (a17 as a dataset scene) and three invalid twins.
  - `test_contract.py` gains a drift test: v2 must equal v1 except its identity text, `schema_version` and the frame's
    conversion. It also gains the version-boundary checks.
- **The adapter**, `grounding/adapters/iref_vla/`:
  - `pinned.py`: Appendix A's manifest and the approved identities.
  - `sources.py`: strict CSV, JSON, number, ID and label reading, and the adapter's errors.
  - `convert.py`: the scene stage, which takes only the object CSV, the region CSV and the vocabulary; commands from
    expression keys; the annotation bundle with its cross-checks; and the graph cross-check.
  - `output.py`: the manifest and report, validation of every output, and atomic publishing.
  - `__main__.py`: the command line, with exits 0, 2 and 3.
- **The annotation bundle's schema:** `schemas/iref-annotations.v1.json`, reference-only, validated by the adapter and
  not registered in the contract's dispatch.
- **Tests:** `grounding/tests/test_iref_vla_adapter.py`, with `grounding/tests/fixtures/iref_vla/`:
  - `expectations.json`;
  - five hand-written source-format fixtures (`mini_*`);
  - a `.gitattributes` that keeps their CRLF bytes exact.
- **Records:**
  - `docs/iref-vla-adapter.md`;
  - `docs/scene-contract.md` (scene v2, its codes, the limits);
  - the indexes;
  - D74, which closes O23;
  - three limitations entries.

## 6. What failed and why

1. **A test bug, caught before freezing.** While writing the test, its repeated-expression check for the mini fixtures
   counted entries wrongly. I caught it on rereading, before the hashes were recorded.
2. **A test gap, found by mutation testing.** Writing straight into the final folder passed the write-failure check,
   because clean-up removed the folder. A killed process would still leave it half-written. One check was added after
   the first run, and labelled so: the final folder must not exist while any file is written. It now catches that
   mutant.
3. **Found, not fixed here.** `schemas/README.md` doesn't list A2.1e's three schemas (`grounding-query.v1.json`,
   `resolver-config.v1.json`, `grounding-result.v1.json`). That's an omission from the A2.1e delivery, outside this
   increment's scope.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

- **Added, as tests or records of approved rules:**
  - a valid v2 contract fixture, three invalid twins and their README rows;
  - the v2/v1 drift test;
  - the update to `docs/scene-contract.md`;
  - three limitations entries.
- **Choices where the brief was silent**, each recorded in D74 and `docs/iref-vla-adapter.md`:
  - the error-code assignment;
  - tests fail, never skip, without the pinned files;
  - commands name the source commit and path but not the statements' hash;
  - an unreadable input is `E_IREF_SOURCE_SHAPE`;
  - an `unknown`-versus-known raw label is a conflict;
  - cross-check tolerances of 1e-9 m and 1e-12 relative;
  - the graph cross-check leaves box corners to the tests;
  - colour-group consistency rules.
