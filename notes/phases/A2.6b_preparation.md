## 1. What it does

`python -m grounding.preparation.iref_vla_dev` has two commands, one for each freeze (correction 2).

**`freeze`** fixes, before any preparation, one hashed specification (`spec.json`) holding:

- the official-first partition and every environment's assignment;
- the cap: 64 parents per environment;
- the eligibility rules;
- the sampling procedure and its salt (`second-eyes/a26/sampling/v1`);
- the desktop subset's rule (salt `second-eyes/a26/desktop/v1`; 32 parents × both views, `coordinates_v2`; 64
  requests);
- the analysis plan: a paired environment-cluster bootstrap, B = 10,000, the literal seed 20261010, 95% percentile
  intervals, weights recomputed in each replicate;
- the closed partitions.

**`prepare`** requires that specification and refuses an inventory whose hash differs from it. It then:

1. **Reads only the development and legacy scenes.** No other scene's zip member is read (`release.read_source(only=…)`).
2. **Runs the accepted chain per scene,** in parallel worker processes, with every scene accounted for, and failures
   recorded:
   - A2.2a's import, under the scene's release identity (`release.import_release_scene`);
   - A2.2c's selection audit;
   - A2.2d's materialization, all with `sample_only=False`.
3. **Resolves each parent's annotation entries** (correction 1).
4. **Applies eligibility.** A parent is eligible under A2.3a's accepted rule (both views materialized, all four
   view-format renderings succeeded), with 3 to 10 objects in both views, and a unique target. Every exclusion keeps its
   reason.
5. **Samples per environment** by the frozen procedure, recording N and n (correction 3).
6. **Builds four D104 requests per selected parent:** both views, both formats, path U, cache off.
7. **Hashes everything:**
   - the request manifest;
   - the sampling record;
   - the desktop subset;
   - review copies of the download receipt, the inventory summary and the partition proposal;
   - the reference-only answers, in their own folder.

## 2. The corrections from ChatGPT's review, as implemented

**1. Multiple entries.** My freeze proposal said all 3,744 multi-entry texts have non-unique targets. That was wrong:
the collector had counted entries, and never compared their targets (`failures.md`). Each parent's entries are now
resolved, independent of their order:

- **The target:** one target over all entries is `unique_target`, counted once, with its annotation IDs. Several
  targets are `conflicting_targets`, quarantined from unique-target scoring.
- **The parsing label:** relation, relation type and anchor sets are compared separately, and give `consistent` or
  `disagreeing`. A unique target is not a unique parsing label.
- **The stratum:** the relation when the entries agree on it; otherwise the explicit stratum `relation_disagreement`.
- Nothing is called semantic ambiguity.

**2. Two freezes.** The partition, cap and procedures are frozen now, and the request manifest after preparation. The
proposal's 2,789 parents and 47 environments were planning estimates. The run reports the final counts:

- eligible and selected parents;
- exclusion reasons;
- contributing environments;
- zero-eligible environments, which are reported, never replaced.

The assigned environments and the salts are fixed. A parent whose four requests do not all build is left out whole, so
the manifest stays matched across formats and views, and its failures are listed.

**3. Weighting.**

- **Sampling.** Strata are filled round-robin, in ascending stratum name, one parent per nonempty stratum per round, up
  to the cap. Within a stratum, parents are taken by ascending SHA-256(salt LF environment LF stratum LF parent).
- **The analysis plan** fixes two weightings:
  - command-weighted: weight N/n, estimating over the eligible development commands;
  - environment-weighted: each environment's eligible relation proportions restored, then equal environment weight.
- The unweighted sample is shown only with that label.

## 3. Changes to accepted code

**The one-scene converter** (`convert.py`, `output.py`) now takes an identity:

- `K` is a proxy over the identity in force;
- `PINNED_IDENTITY` holds A2.2a's exact constants and its four descriptive strings.

**Proof:**

- importing the pinned sample with the original and the refactored code gives 1,942 files, none differing by SHA-256;
- A2.2a's suite passes 96 of 96, and A2.2c's passes 83 of 83 on the real sample;
- A2.2d's 53 unit checks pass. Its real-sample stage had no failure before the sandbox's time limit.

**The A2.5 code is unchanged.** The D104 request record mirrors `prompt_goldens._golden`, with the format as a
parameter. The relations format takes A2.2d's relation work cap, from each bundle's own summary (1,190), as A2.2d and
A2.3d pass it.

## 5. The first preparation run (RTX PC, 10 October), superseded before inference

**The run.**

- **Specification:** `spec-20261010-193912`, SHA-256 `9fa980010eb4…`, freezing the inventory `87bacea94550…`.
- **Preparation:** `prep-20261010-193912`, with 12 workers.
- **Review archive:** `a26b-review-20261010-193912.zip`, SHA-256 `aa4ef1b7152f93a3d12d34766f0b7ec6246b666c66e13001e679ab7483dddd32`.

**Its counts:**

- **Scenes:** 100 of 104 prepared.
- **Development:** 46,442 parents, 24,227 eligible (52.2%).
- **Legacy:** 3,922 parents, 1,629 eligible.
- **Annotation entries:** every parent's entries name one target: 50,364 unique targets and no conflicting ones. 297
  parsing labels disagree, all on anchors under one relation: 279 in development and 18 in legacy (corrected; an earlier
  version of this note put all 297 under development).
- **Selected:** 2,651 development parents and 64 legacy; 2,715 parents with all four requests, 10,860 requests, no
  request failure.
- **The desktop subset:** 64 requests.
- **Zero-eligible:** one environment, `scannet:scene0414`. Its 53 commands are 13 unparsed and 40 over budget; it is
  reported, not replaced.
- **Contributing:** 46 development environments, and the legacy one.

**Claude's independent check of the archive:**

- the specification and the inventory hash as frozen;
- every manifest hash reads back;
- the official lists equal their pins;
- four matched requests per parent, with unique IDs;
- the desktop subset is exactly the 32 lowest salted hashes;
- the whole selection, re-derived from the eligibility rows with the frozen procedure, equals the recorded one (2,715
  parents), and so do every stratum's N and n;
- tokens per request: 1,573 to 3,211 for `coordinates_v2`, and 1,930 to 4,992 for the relations format.

**Why it was superseded.** Four scenes failed:

- **`scene0193_01` has no statements in the release.** A2.2a's reader refuses an empty expression object. Such scenes
  are now recorded as `no_statements`, without running the chain.
- **`scene0207_02`, `scene0307_02` and `scene0633_01` failed when Windows refused the final rename of a freshly
  written folder** ("Access is denied": a file lock, typically the antivirus scanner or the search indexer). Nothing in
  the data caused it, but it removed one scan from each of three development environments, and so changed their
  eligible pools and samples.

**The fix.** A stage that fails on such a lock, having published nothing, is retried up to three times, with pauses of
2 and 6 seconds, and each retry is recorded. Data errors are never retried. The preparation is rerun under the same
frozen specification. No inference had happened, so its request manifest replaces this one, which is kept as
superseded evidence.

**A finding for the review: the eligible population is shaped by the accepted parser's coverage.**

- The parser's 25 aliases (`evaluation/iref_vla/protocol.v1.json`) cover closest, farthest, between, near, on and below.
  They have no phrase for "above" or "in".
- So none of the 428 "above" and 69 "in" development commands is eligible.
- "near" is 24% eligible (44% unparsed, 24% too few objects), "below" 19% and "on" 26% (each about 40% too few
  objects). The distance relations are 64–65% eligible, and "between" 47% (21% over budget).
- The baseline therefore describes the eligible population, as the specification states, and is silent on "above" and
  "in". Widening the parser would change the frozen eligibility; that is a decision for the review.

## 6. The final preparation run (RTX PC, 10 October), accepted

**The run.**

- **Preparation:** `prep-20261010-211017`, under the same specification `9fa98001…`, with the transient-lock retry
  (`39e7969`).
- **Review archive:** `a26b-review-20261010-211017.zip`, SHA-256
  `e3c620f527f34b715ea1651b693c11abe76493e16a7df9ed91be69a5ab1744fc`.
- **Request manifest:** `requests.jsonl`, SHA-256 `dc725cfe7bfab1b80a468b748d12a8b21b5583f14176d43337a0835c1b6f03ea`.

**Its counts:**

- **Scenes:** 103 prepared, and `scene0193_01` recorded as `no_statements`. Five scenes were retried after a transient
  lock at import: `scene0100_02`, `scene0307_01`, `scene0378_00`, `scene0606_01` and `scene0606_02`.
- **Development:** 50,957 parents (equal to the inventory), 26,672 eligible (52.3%), all with unique targets.
- **Disagreeing parsing labels:** 279 development parents and 18 legacy (297 in all). 15 of the selected development
  parents carry one. They are kept for unique-target selection scoring, and the flag is preserved in the scores.
- **Selected:** 2,651 development parents, from 46 contributing environments: 35 at the cap of 64, and 11 below it (8 to
  59). Zero-eligible: `scannet:scene0414`.
- **Legacy:** 3,922 parents, 1,629 eligible, 64 selected.
- **Requests:** 10,860, with no failure. Token medians are 1,953 for `coordinates_v2` and 2,533 for the relations format
  (maxima 3,211 and 4,992).
- **The desktop subset:** 64 requests.

**Claude's independent check:**

- every hash as frozen;
- four matched requests per parent;
- the desktop subset exactly the 32 lowest salted hashes;
- the selection re-derived, with every N and n equal;
- every retry a transient lock on a prepared scene;
- **determinism on real data:** the 43 environments that the first run's failures did not touch have identical
  selections in both runs, and the three affected ones changed only where their pools grew (44, 47 and 57 of 64
  unchanged).

**Reading the preparation's own report** (corrected in ChatGPT's review; the frozen files are unchanged):

- **"Contributing environments: 47" includes the legacy environment.** Development has 46 contributing environments,
  of 47 assigned; `scannet:scene0414` is zero-eligible.
- **The exclusion-reason totals are view-level occurrences, not parents:** 37,256 unparsed, 5,565 over budget and 3,573
  too few. By parent, development has 17,196 unparsed, 3,743 over budget and 3,346 too few; a parent may have several
  reasons. 24,285 development parents are excluded in all, which is 50,957 minus 26,672.
- The report's wording is corrected in `dev.py` for any future preparation: environments by partition; reason totals
  labelled as view-level occurrences, beside parent-level counts; disagreements by partition and among the selected.

**Accepted.** ChatGPT's A2.6c brief accepted this preparation and fixed its specification and request-file hashes for
the baseline runs. Its coverage finding stands with every result: the accepted parser has no "above" or "in"; "near" is
24% eligible.
