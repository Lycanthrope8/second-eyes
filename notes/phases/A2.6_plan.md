## 1. What A2.6 is, and is not

**What it is.** D97 placed A2.6 between the Quest integration and fine-tuning. It assesses the base model, in its
accepted operating configuration, and the reference baselines on development data, so that A2.4 has a measured
baseline to beat, and evidence for its data and format choices.

**What it is not:**

- **Not preliminary Gate B.** That is the supplement's later step: frozen checkpoints, opened lab commands and
  advisor-agreed thresholds (O20). No Gate B status is inferred from this assessment.
- **The final held-out lab commands stay closed,** throughout development, selection and calibration.
- **No perspective claim:** O27 remains open.
- **No claim of numerical acceptance.** Uncached and cached numerical acceptance remain unresolved (D105). Where the
  deployed path's decisions are reported, they sit beside the float32 reference, described, not accepted.

**The accepted operating configuration** is carried forward unchanged:

- Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct Q8_0, GGUF `dd753cd6…`;
- llama.cpp b11277 (`eae11d22`), headset library `c366cf34…`;
- 8,192 tokens of context, 2 threads, flags 0;
- path U with prefix caching off;
- the D104 prompt asset, its fixed serialized-order mapping, and choices before the scene;
- D56 and D101 as written.

## 2. The constraint that shapes it: one published scene

All development evidence so far comes from one IRef-VLA sample scene, ScanNet `scene0010_01`:

- A2.2b's rules baseline;
- A2.3a to A2.3e, and A2.3d's comparison of rules, a 0.5B and a 7B model;
- A2.5's goldens.

That scene yields 1,004 eligible commands over sub-scenes. A2.2a deferred held-out splits (section 8 of its note).

**What the plan requires that one scene can't provide:**

- The supplement's step 6 asks for uncertainty over the number of independent scenes and commands.
- Its step 4 needs separate training, development, calibration and test partitions, and transfer to unseen development
  scenes.

So A2.6 must first widen the development data to many scenes, with scene-disjoint partitions frozen before any model
sees the development partition. Until then, A2.3d's numbers remain one scene's numbers.

## 3. The proposed steps

**A2.6a · Data and partitions.**

- Acquire a multi-scene published resource through the existing IRef-VLA adapter (A2.2a), with access, licence and
  provenance records. ScanNet-derived scenes carry ScanNet's terms. Record the filtering counts.
- Write one scene-disjoint partition manifest: training, development, calibration and test, by a salted SHA-256 of the
  scene ID, with the shares to be decided (proposed: 70, 10, 10, 10).
- Freeze and hash the manifest before any request is materialized. The test partition stays closed, as do the lab
  commands.

**A2.6b · Inputs.** Materialize the development partition with the accepted A2.2d preparation:

- the coordinates_v2 serializer (A2.5's format), and the second candidate format if it is kept (decision 4);
- the D104 fixed mapping, for the operating configuration.

**A2.6c · Systems,** on the same development requests:

1. the rules (A2.2b), in both inventory views;
2. the 0.5B in float32, with eager attention: the PC reference, on the A2.3 protocol;
3. the accepted operating configuration on the desktop host: Q8_0, path U, the same build and settings. Its decisions
   are reported beside the reference, with agreement figures. This is not D101 acceptance;
4. the 7B in float32 on the RTX PC, as the larger reference;
5. the floors: always B, and the second position.

**A2.6d · Metrics,** on the development partition only, as the supplement lists them:

- correct targets, wrong selections, invalid outputs and clarifications (ASK);
- a clarification on a uniquely answerable command is not a correct selection;
- ambiguous and unsupported commands are scored separately;
- errors broken down by relation, frame, composition and candidate count;
- uncertainty from a bootstrap clustered by scene (scenes as the independent unit), with the command counts;
- the inventory views reported separately, never as one headline number.

**A2.6e · Report and handoff to A2.4:**

- the baseline table, and an error analysis;
- the two formats to shortlist (the supplement: "zero-shot rank is not evidence of post-training rank");
- the frozen partition manifest that A2.4 trains and selects on.

## 4. Decisions needed before implementation

1. **The data resource and scope.** The full IRef-VLA release, or a bounded multi-scene subset? Which source datasets,
   with which licence terms accepted? How many development scenes are targeted?
2. **The partition rule and shares.** Is calibration needed in A2.6, or only in A2.4?
3. **The systems.** Include the deployed Q8_0 path U on the desktop host? Include the 7B on the RTX PC?
4. **The formats.** coordinates_v2 only (the deployed format), or both A2.3d candidates?
5. **The uncertainty method:** the bootstrap's design and the reported intervals.
6. **The compute budget.** One full path-U evaluation takes about 64 s on the desktop host (r013), so 1,000 requests
   would be about 18 hours. A practical split might run the float32 reference and the 7B over the whole development
   partition on the RTX PC, and the deployed path over a fixed, hash-chosen subset, for agreement only.
7. **Confirmation:** the lab commands, and the test partition, stay closed throughout.

## 5. Not in A2.6

- Fine-tuning (A2.4).
- Gate B (supplement §11, O20).
- The lab commands, and the test partition.
- Perspective results (O27).
- Numerical-acceptance claims.
- Headset runs, which are not needed for a development assessment on the PC.

## 6. Corrections from ChatGPT's review (10 October), adopted

These supersede the earlier sections wherever they differ.

1. **Split by physical environment and ancestry,** not simply by scene ID.
   - Rescans, regions, derived examples, and copies of one place across datasets stay together, in one group.
   - The source's official splits are preserved where they apply.
   - The group identity is hashed, with the salt fixed before any selection.
   - The inspected `scene0010_01`, with its related scans, forms a separately labelled **legacy-development** group. It
     goes in no new partition: not calibration, not test, and not the unseen-scene baseline.
2. **The adapter is a one-scene adapter.** `pinned.py` supports exactly the pinned sample. Multi-scene work goes through
   a **versioned extension**. That extension preserves the accepted sample's behaviour, validates new source identities,
   geometry, vocabulary and annotation correspondence, and never weakens the pin checks.
3. **The desktop Q8_0 is not the deployed Quest configuration.** It uses the same GGUF, but a different native binary,
   architecture and computation path. It is labelled the **desktop Q8_0/U comparison**, and its agreement with float32
   says nothing about the Quest's agreement. No headset run is needed in this phase.
4. **Both formats are kept for A2.4:** `coordinates_v2` and `coordinates_relations_v2`. Both use matched examples,
   inventory views and the same D104 mapping. A2.6 measures their baseline behaviour and cost, and eliminates neither for
   a lower zero-shot score. The D104 mapping differs from A2.3d's randomized mappings, so these are **new, versioned
   requests with fresh float32 outputs.** That does not reopen the ordering experiment.
5. **Denominators and uncertainty are explicit.**
   - Independent physical environments, and unique parent commands, are counted separately from format and view
     variants.
   - The bootstrap resamples physical-environment groups, paired across the systems compared.
   - Each headline metric states whether it weights commands or scenes equally.
   - Unsupported and ambiguous cases need trustworthy labels: neither a low score nor an ASK establishes ambiguity.
   - A breakdown that cannot be computed is reported as unavailable.
6. **No redundant floors; compute in actual requests.**
   - With D104's ordered mapping, "always B" and "second object position" are normally the same baseline. Their
     equivalence is reported, and they are not presented as two floors.
   - The budget counts actual model requests: formats × views × systems × commands.
   - The 64 s per evaluation is a measured, case-dependent desktop cost, not a universal rate.

## 7. Choices adopted for A2.6

These are ChatGPT's recommendations, adopted by the project lead.

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Data | a bounded multi-scene IRef-VLA subset, once access and compatibility are established. No unrelated datasets, and no unnecessary raw imagery: only the four text files per scene are read, never the `.ply` clouds. |
| Partitions | the official restrictions and splits first. Where a project split is needed, 70/10/10/10 by physical-environment group, provisionally; the resulting counts are inspected before freezing. |
| Calibration | reserved for later; not used in this assessment |
| Systems | the existing rules, the pinned 0.5B in float32 and the pinned 7B in float32. The 7B is an empirical reference, not an assumed ceiling. |
| Formats and views | both formats; the full-inventory and source-known views reported separately, with equivalent evidence across systems in each condition |
| Uncertainty | a paired cluster bootstrap by physical environment, with a fixed seed and a declared interval procedure. Scene counts are reported, with no strong population claim from a small convenience subset. |
| Desktop Q8_0/U | a bounded, preselected comparison subset, not a full CPU sweep. Its request cap is fixed before inference, once the data and costs are known. |
| Closed data | calibration, the public test and the final lab-test commands stay outside development selection and assessment |

## 8. A2.6a: the data inventory and the partition foundation (next delivery)

It establishes:

1. the accessible release and its terms, and the independent environment groups available;
2. retained and rejected counts with reasons, including compatibility with the target-independent 3-to-10-object
   preparation;
3. the versioned adapter extension, with regression checks;
4. the grouping and split manifest, with the legacy sample excluded from the untouched partitions;
5. the coverage of trustworthy target, relation, anchor and frame annotations, for A2.4's direct-selection and parsing
   designs;
6. exact development caps for scenes, commands and requests, and the resulting compute estimate.

The concrete run specification is frozen only after this, before any inference.
