**What fix 1 changes:**
- **The audit.** Commands quarantined before their view checks (a disagreeing parsing label, for example) are shown
  with their visible objects and marked not checked. These caused the crash at the end of Part 2b.
- **`prepare --reuse-work`.** Each scan already prepared in `a24-20261012-032009\work` is reused only if all twelve of
  its own checks pass:
  - its three stages are complete;
  - today's importer reproduces its import byte for byte;
  - its recorded inputs, code, libraries, model description, tokenizer and runtime are today's;
  - its outputs are unchanged, checked by the accepted bundle verifier.

  A scan that fails any check stops the run before anything is published, naming the scan; nothing in the work folder
  is changed. Labels, cohorts, examples, the audit and the specification are recomputed.
- **The tests:** the fixture gains disagreeing labels; a verified reuse must reproduce a fresh preparation exactly; and
  tampered copies must be refused.
