## 1. What was built

Offline only (D68): `grounding/relations/directions.py`, which provides `left`, `right`, `in_front_of` and
`behind` in the `user_heading`, `user_to_anchor` and `object_intrinsic` frames, chosen by the caller. It comes with:
- an immutable provisional configuration, `directions.v1.json`, in the format `schemas/direction-config.v1.json`;
- record-scoped field provenance in each result;
- the brief's fixed cases with contract-valid fixtures (`grounding/tests/fixtures/directions/`);
- the test `grounding/tests/test_directions.py`;
- `docs/directions.md`.

Not included: A2.1b's code and tests, which are unchanged; a resolver, serializer, adapter, training, or Unity, C#
or headset code.

## 2. Final configuration

`directions.v1`, identity `directions.v1@73590e939d4f`, status provisional:
- band 0.02 m;
- viewer–anchor cutoff 0.02 m;
- semantic-front horizontal-norm cutoff 0.10.

No runs: nothing was measured on the headset.

## 3. Key numbers

The cases were transcribed into `cases.json` before `directions.py` existed.

`python grounding/tests/test_directions.py`: 105 checks, all passed in the assistant's sandbox (Python 3.12.3;
jsonschema 4.26.0 and 4.0.1):
- the 43 fixed rows, as 90 predicate calls, one check each, all matching on the first run;
- 15 cross-cutting checks.

Of the computed scores and measures, all but one equal the brief's arithmetic exactly. The exception is D17's front
score, 2.2e-16 from 0 (from subtracting 3.8 − 3 and 3.4 − 4), far inside the 1e-9 tolerance and the band.

The accepted suites still pass unchanged: 68 of 68 relation checks and 70 of 70 contract checks. Five deliberate
mistakes, tried outside the suite, were each caught:
- right taken from the front axis in `user_to_anchor`;
- a band edge counted as TRUE;
- an unnormalized heading;
- either cutoff made exclusive.

The run on JH's PC is the acceptance run.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

None from the brief. Routine details it left open:
- When evidence is missing and a frame is also degenerate, both reasons are listed. The value is UNKNOWN with no
  score either way.
- The viewer–anchor separation or the front's horizontal norm is reported whenever it could be computed, including
  in degenerate and missing-evidence cases.
- A record passed in the wrong role raises a `ValueError`: a scene as a command, or a command as a scene.
- Configuration numbers that overflow a float fail to load, like NaN.
- The strict parser is A2.1b's own, reused unchanged.

## 8. Open issues handed to the next phase

- Frame choice, frame plausibility, and the head, body or pointing convention for the user's frame: later, with the
  resolver and the headset's heading source.
- Calibration of the band and cutoffs on development data.
- Compatibility with datasets' directional labels: with the adapter.
- Python 3.9 compatibility is intended but was not executed; only 3.12.3 was run.
- The C# port, its cost on the Quest, and floating-point parity near transformed boundaries: A2.5.
