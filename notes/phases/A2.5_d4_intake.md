## 1. D99 clause by clause

**(1) One request path for the ADB inbox and the panel's presets.**

- Both become a `Ticket` in one `SessionCore` and run through delivery 3's pipeline, as `ExecutionPurpose.Operational`
  with `CacheMode.Off`.
- There is no keyboard, ADB is development transport only, and all grounding stays on the headset.

**(2) The inbox protocol.**

- The PC pushes `files/interactive/inbox/(ID).json.tmp`, then renames it to `(ID).json` on the headset (`adb shell mv`).
  The headset reads only `*.json`, so it never sees a partial file.
- The request record is `a25_interactive_request`, format 1. It carries:
  - a request ID matching `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.-]{0,63}$`;
  - `expected_scene`: the snapshot ID, scene ID, scene revision, and the SHA-256 of the snapshot file;
  - the command record, whose scene ID and revision must equal `expected_scene`'s;
  - `command_kind`: `dataset_command` or `written`.
- **Each ID is processed once per session.** It is claimed in a ledger before anything else. A later file with the
  same ID gets only `outbox/(ID).duplicate-(time).json`, and the first acknowledgement and result stand.
- **The answers go under the same ID:**
  - `outbox/(ID).ack.json`: accepted, or rejected with a reason;
  - `outbox/(ID).result.json`: the outcome with its stage times.
  - Both are written to a temporary file and renamed.
- **Rejected explicitly, as `refused` outcomes:**
  - `invalid_request`, a schema failure, with its reason;
  - `stale_scene_binding`, when the expected scene differs from the current one; the differing field is named;
  - `no_scene_bound`.
- **Unanswerable files.** A file without a usable request ID can't be answered under one. It is kept in
  `inbox/processed/` and logged as an event.
- Every processed file moves to `inbox/processed/`, as evidence.

**(3) Timing**, on one headset stopwatch:

- the stages: detected, validated, queued, inference start, inference end, outcome;
- `app_observed_ms` = outcome − detected;
- for presets, "detected" is the button event, and for the inbox it is the file's detection.

The PC's push-and-rename time is in the PC's receipt only, and is never combined with the headset's clock.

**D98: cancellation and stale-result rejection.**

- **Cancel** answers queued requests at once (`cancelled_while_queued`). A native evaluation in progress cannot be
  interrupted without a native change, so its finished result becomes `cancelled` (`cancelled_during_evaluation`).
  The target is discarded, and the earlier status is kept in the detail.
- **Switching the scene** starts a new epoch. A result for the previous epoch becomes `refused`
  (`stale_result_scene_changed`), its target discarded.
- **Neither rule touches a technical failure,** which keeps its own reason, and neither turns anything into an ASK.

**(5) Scenes and presets.**

- The scenes are the three annotated golden snapshots (3, 6 and 10 objects), read through the golden check's
  hash-checked reader.
- The presets are their golden dataset commands. Written fixtures are not presets: they are implementation checks only.

## 2. On the headset

The panel has a new row:

- **Session (A2.5 d4):** start or end. Starting loads the model once (8,192 tokens, 2 threads, flags 0) and starts the
  inbox and the worker.
- **Scene:** cycles through the three snapshots.
- **Preset:** sends the current scene's next dataset command.
- **Cancel.**

The text box lists the scene's objects with their letters (D104's serialized order, K last), marks the chosen one with
an arrow, and shows the last outcome and its app-observed time. The status line names the request, its outcome, its
time and its execution path.

Each session writes `files/interactive/sessions/(UTC time)/`: `session.json`, and `outcomes.jsonl` with every answered
request, presets included.

Its events are `interactive.session.start`, `interactive.scene`, `interactive.result`,
`interactive.inbox.unanswerable`, `interactive.inbox.error` and `interactive.worker.error`.

## 7. ChatGPT's A2.5 review (10 October): the shutdown and presentation races

The review examined `bbb0ffe` and found two races the headset runs could not exercise.

**Shutdown.**

- **Before:** `EndAsync` cancelled the inbox without awaiting its loop, then drained the queue. A handler already
  running could enqueue afterwards. `Finish` also cleared `Running` before `Publish`, so shutdown could close the log
  before the last result was recorded.
- **Now:**
  - admission closes first (`SessionCore.Close`): a late handler's `Admit` fails, and it answers its own request as
    `session_ended`;
  - the loop tasks are kept, and shutdown awaits the inbox loop, then the worker;
  - the worker releases a ticket (`Release`) only after publishing it, and exits once admission is closed and nothing
    is left;
  - the log and the model close only after that.

**Presentation.**

- **Before:** a result could pass the worker's stale check and reach the main thread after a scene switch. The scene
  view could also show an old outcome's object mapping under the current scene's heading.
- **Now:** at the UI boundary, a result is presented only if it comes from the current session (each ticket carries
  its session stamp) and the current scene epoch (`Presentation.Decide`). Otherwise it is logged, not presented. The
  scene view always uses the current scene's own D104 mapping (`Presentation.SceneLines`), and marks a target only for
  a presentable result.

**Deterministic tests:** 12 added, 57 C# checks in all.

- the interleaving of a running ticket, a queued one, closing, and a late admission;
- a finished but unpublished ticket keeping shutdown waiting;
- drained only after release;
- presentation by session and epoch;
- the current mapping over an old outcome;
- the pipeline's top-level boundary.

A short headset acceptance sequence for the changed startup and lifecycle behaviour, with D102 after the rebuild,
follows.

## 8. The shutdown timeout, and the correction acceptance (10 October)

**The defect,** found by ChatGPT's review of `52686fb`.

- `EndAsync` waited with `Task.WhenAny(task, Task.Delay(…))`, then disposed regardless of which finished. A timeout
  could therefore close the log, or end the session, with publication outstanding.
- The panel's error path also called `Dispose()` at once, and reset the session row. That would allow another model
  load while work could still be active.

**The correction,** the smallest that meets the ordering:

- **`SessionShutdown.RunAsync`,** in the pure intake core:
  - closes admission and answers the queue;
  - stops the inbox;
  - then waits on the inbox task and the worker task until each has actually completed.
  - The periodic delay only reports progress: it never authorizes disposal.
  - A faulted or cancelled task, or a request left unreleased, makes the report not clean.
  - Nothing in it throws before both tasks have completed.
- **`InteractiveService.EndAsync`** runs it, then writes the session's closing record (`session-end.json` and an
  `interactive.session.end` event: clean or not, faults, answered count). Only then does it close the log and unload.
  It never throws.
- **The panel** awaits it without disposing. If ending ever fails unexpectedly, the row stays locked rather than allowing
  a new model load.

**Deterministic tests:** 7 added, 64 C# checks in all.

- A pending worker gives only reported timeouts, and shutdown completes only after publication and release.
- A late inbox handler's refusal is published before completion.
- Faulted and cancelled tasks are not clean.
- A finished but unreleased request is not clean.
- A failure while answering the queue still waits for the worker.

**The harness is committed** in `tools/csharp-tests/`: Behavior, UnityGlue and PanelPartial, on .NET 8 at C# 9, with
no packages. Unity still compiles `ChatPanel.cs` itself, and the Android build.

**The acceptance procedure:** cases A to D, in two sessions, per ChatGPT's brief.

- `acceptance-check` verifies the raw records, not the pull summary:
  - both sessions and their startup checks, ordered before serving in the event log;
  - every sent request answered exactly once, in its acknowledging session, and identical in that session's log;
  - no other request counted;
  - no target on a cancelled or refused outcome;
  - path U with nothing kept;
  - the golden prompt and mapping identities;
  - each session's closing record, clean and after its last result;
  - the pull's and D102's hashes;
  - the source identity, and the operator checklist.
- `inbox-send --wait` and `inbox-status` let the operator confirm each case's condition at once, and retry with fresh IDs
  if needed.
