## 1. What was built

Round 1: `se_load_ex`'s options, batched scoring (`se_score_many`), stripped Android builds, and four command-line
configurations that settled O18 (D57, D58). Round 2: `LlamaNative` and `LlamaRuntime` (one worker thread for every
native call), the panel's Runtime choice, `model.scores`, `push-model`, and checks that judge answers and scores
together (D58).

## 2. Final configuration

llama.cpp `b11277` (`eae11d22`), flash attention automatic, weights repacked, memory-mapped, 16 sequences in one cache,
candidates scored in one batch, 2 threads (D59). Model `qwen2.5-0.5b-instruct-q8_0.gguf` (531 MB) in the app's data
folder. Commit `95d7e4fd` (the runs are marked dirty: the plugin's and new scripts' `.meta` files came later). Runs
`20260930_A1_r032` (functional check), `20260930_A1_r033`–`20261001_A1_r036` (runs 9–12).

D57 (the two rounds), D58 (configuration; O18 closed), D59 (2 threads).

## 6. What failed and why

Four threads: 100 times slower per token, explained by the three cores the app's threads get (D59). The summary
flagged loads as memory jumps; four runs kept the placeholder purpose. Both tools fixed.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

Runs 10–12 were recorded from before Load, by the operator's choice, to capture the load's cost; their means include
the load's first seconds. Run 12 changed threads before Load, as planned.
