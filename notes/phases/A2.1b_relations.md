## 1. What was built

The relation library v1, offline only (D67): `grounding/relations/geometry.py` (box geometry, footprints, overlap,
exact box distance, containment) and `predicates.py` (configuration, three-valued comparisons and logic, cached
geometry with field-level provenance, near, above, below, on, inside, between, and closest and farthest with joint
eligibility); its provisional configuration `relations.v1.json` and format `schemas/relation-config.v1.json`; three
contract-valid fixture scenes and the reviewed cases (`grounding/tests/fixtures/relations/`); the test
`grounding/tests/test_relations.py`; and `docs/relations.md`. No directions, resolver, serializer, adapter, training
or headset code.

## 2. Final configuration

`relations.v1` (identity `relations.v1@d922fe902669`), status provisional: the initial values of the approved
proposal. Pure Python plus jsonschema. No runs: this increment measures nothing on the headset, so no run IDs.

## 3. Key numbers

`python grounding/tests/test_relations.py`: 68 checks, all passed in the assistant's sandbox (Python 3.12.3;
jsonschema 4.26.0 and 4.0.1), in about 0.3 s. All 47 reviewed cases matched their hand-calculated values on the first
run; the largest difference was 5.5e-9 (case 15's tilt, from the fixture's rounded quaternion). The contract suite
still passes, 70 of 70. Two deliberate mistakes, tried outside the suite, were both caught: ignoring rotations turns
case 34 into FALSE at 0.60 m, and one-at-a-time eligibility turns case 29 into no_match. The run on JH's PC is the
acceptance run.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

Details the approved proposal left to the implementation:

- Any relation called with a repeated object is FALSE (`not_distinct`), as between was defined.
- Quaternions are normalized before use; validation already bounds their norm to 1e-6.
- Measures are kept for TRUE, FALSE and UNKNOWN results alike, for calibration later; reasons are empty for TRUE.
- A configuration's identity hashes its parsed content, not its bytes, so Windows and Linux line endings give the
  same identity.
- A reused cache key with different geometry raises an error instead of returning stale geometry.
- Added to the tests: a configuration with bad values is rejected, an exact tie at the tolerance links, and sampling
  diagnostics mix intersecting and separated boxes.
