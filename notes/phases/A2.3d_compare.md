**Findings.**
- **Rules.** Their full-inventory failures reflect unknown-category and anchor uncertainty: all 254 unresolved records
  carry `uncertain_anchor` and `unknown_category`. In the source-known view they resolve 253, all to the published
  target, and 3 are ambiguous (two multiple definite matches, one rank tie).
- **The 7B is ASK-dominated** (917 of 1,024 choices), also in the source-known view where the rules resolve the
  published target.
- **The 0.5B chooses B or C** in 973 of 1,024 requests (95.0%; B 721, C 252) and shows no demonstrated advantage over
  the simple reference policies.
- **Both formats remain candidates.** No format winner, general model ranking, Gate B pass or deployment-readiness claim
  follows.
