## Measured, not judged (as corrected in the review)

From three parser sessions on A2.5's 3-, 6- and 10-object snapshots, run `20261014_A2_r024`.

**Definitions:**
- **Submission** was the panel's **Preset** button, which sends the scene's next golden dataset command, not typed
  text. Typing in the headset was not measured.
- **The endpoint** is the validated decision (a target or a decline), before the panel displays it. It is not the
  moment the result becomes visible.
- **Cold** is the first request after the session has loaded and checked the model. It excludes session startup.
  **Warm** is every later request.

**The figures:**
- **Latency:** cold median 1.233 s (n 3); warm median 1.230 s, 90th percentile 1.286 s (n 54). Warm medians by scene:
  1.18, 1.28 and 1.22 s for 3, 6 and 10 objects (n 6, 18 and 30).
- **Stages:** decoding median 1.18 s; resolution median 17.36 ms.
- **Tokens:** 154 to 156 prompt tokens; 9 to 12 generated (median 10, the end token included).
- **Session startup:** about 10 s, mostly the model's SHA-256 check; the load itself took 1.1 to 1.5 s.
- **Memory:** about 1.6 GB resident after the load (the wrapper's VmRSS and VmHWM; peak 1,604,028 kB).
- **Outcomes:** 57 requests: 27 selections and 30 ASK, none technical.
- **Consistency:** all 15 golden commands (3, 6 and 6 per scene) gave identical results in every session. The 10
  presses on the 10-object scene repeated 4 of its 6 commands, adding 12 warm samples.

**Frame behaviour** (OVR Metrics, aligned to the app's event log by the project's method: offset about −0.73 s, plausible
range −0.85 to −0.60 s):
- about **72 fps during request processing**;
- in the one-second buckets overlapping requests, **11 stale frames**, around the first-result hitch, and CPU about
  55 to 57%.

These buckets also contain work besides decoding, so they don't attribute stale frames to inference (O26). The
whole-recording figures include the session starts (O28).

**Heat:** SoC peak 63.8 °C, thermal status 0 throughout, plugged in.

**Outside the sessions:**
- the memory increase 28 minutes into the recording was the chat panel's own base-model load, after all sessions had
  ended;
- the Parser check paused while the headset was off. Its timings show no trace of the pause (6.1 minutes of work on
  the monotonic clock).

## Known issues

- **O28:** each parser session's start blocks Unity's main thread for 350 to 470 ms. The parser folder is loaded and
  verified synchronously, which caused 97 of the recording's 143 stale frames. The fix, for the next app change:
  - verify off the main thread, keeping every integrity check;
  - set the starting state and disable conflicting controls before awaiting;
  - verify the change on Quest.
- **O29:** a first-result hitch, one 160 ms frame during the first request of the app's first parser session.
- **O14** stays open: graphics memory grows when the headset comes off and on while the app runs.

## What this establishes, and what it doesn't

A2.7 establishes **deployment fidelity for the tested parser path**:
- the exported model decodes on the Quest exactly as on the desktop, and as the float32 reference;
- the C# resolution path reproduces Python exactly.

It does not establish:
- general language accuracy, or clarification quality;
- automatic perception of the surroundings: the scenes are dataset snapshots;
- support for viewpoint-dependent commands: parser v1 has no frames (D116).

There is no Gate B, and no Quest numerical acceptance beyond criteria 4 and 7 for this candidate.
