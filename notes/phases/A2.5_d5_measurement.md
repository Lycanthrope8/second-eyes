# A2.5 delivery 5: the measurement run

Status: **measured.** The compiled result is section 7, corrected in section 8. A2.5's closing statuses are in `A2.5_closing.md`.

## 1. Scope

The scope comes from D98 ("measured resources") and D99(3) and (7). The choices are mine, for ChatGPT's review.

**One operational session on the headset,** on path U only (cache off, prefix reuse refused):

- every golden dataset command of the 3-, 6- and 10-object snapshots through the ADB inbox (26 in all), with the scene
  switched on the panel between snapshots;
- presets on the first scene;
- the two behaviours delivery 4 has not yet shown on the headset: cancel while queued, and a stale result after a scene
  switch.

**Measured, per D99(3):** each request's stages on the headset clock (detected, validated, queued, inference start and
end, outcome) and the app-observed latency. That is detection to outcome for the inbox, and button event to outcome for
presets. The pipeline's own stage times (build, tokenize, evaluate, score) and the tokens are measured too.

**Memory, as the runtime reports it (D99(7)):**

- llama.cpp's own buffer lines in the session's load log: the model, its repacked copy, the KV cache and the compute
  buffer;
- the wrapper's resident and peak memory (`se_memory_kb`) before and after the load, and after every answered request.

**The PC's push-and-rename times** are reported apart, on the PC clock, and never combined with the headset's.

**Optional: frames and heat,** with A1's existing `tools/profile.py record`, as `docs/profiling.md` describes. It saves
thermal state, battery state and the process list every 30 seconds, and the OVR Metrics CSV: frame rate, stale frames,
CPU and GPU load, app memory.

**Descriptive only:** for golden dataset commands, whether the choice equals the D2 CPU float32 baseline's. No accuracy
claim (D98), and no numerical acceptance.

## 2. The tools

- **Headset:** the delivery-4 session now loads with llama.cpp's load log captured (`startup-log.txt`, verbose during
  the load only). `session.json` records the memory around the load, and every answered request records resident and
  peak memory. `outbox-pull` also brings the load log.
- **`inbox-batch --run RUN --goldens G --snapshot 3|6|10`** sends every golden dataset command of the snapshot, in
  order. The IDs are `m3-01`, `m6-01` and so on.
- **`measure-report --run RUN --goldens G --references REFS`** writes `raw/measure/<UTC time>/summary.json` and
  `report.md`, covering:
  - per scene size and intake: status counts, and app-observed, inference and queue times (median, 90th percentile,
    max);
  - stage times and tokens;
  - memory;
  - the PC's push times;
  - the descriptive agreement.
- **Tests:** `test_quest_measure.py`, 10 checks, worked by hand.

## 4. The first attempt (run `20261009_A2_r018`, 9 October): incomplete, kept as evidence

Evidence zip `043a6d83ee22c6fba66f090cad8480ea24443846c417985b751d9de16cfbd93e`, session `20261009-215006`.

**Established:**

- The identity check ran after the delivery-5 install, at a new APK path, and passed.
- **Memory, as llama.cpp reports it at load:**
  - mapped model 500.79 MiB, repacked model 500.52 MiB;
  - KV cache 96.00 MiB, compute buffer 300.25 MiB, output 9.27 MiB.
- **Memory, as the wrapper reports it:**
  - resident 406,660 kB before the load and 1,574,972 kB after it, with a peak of 1,578,648 kB;
  - resident after requests from 1,450,852 to 1,489,316 kB.
- **The two delivery-4 behaviours** missing on the headset since r017:
  - cancel answered seven waiting requests as `cancelled_while_queued` (two from the batch, five presets), and the
    running one (m3-06) as `cancelled_during_evaluation`;
  - a preset whose scene was switched while it ran was refused as `stale_result_scene_changed`.

**Not established: the latency measurement.**

- **The batches queued.** `inbox-batch` sent all requests at once, so they waited in the headset's queue, and their
  app-observed times include up to 41 s of queueing.
- **The steps overlapped.** The laptop returned at once, with no sign of when a batch was answered. Presets and scene
  switches therefore landed mid-batch.
- **Stale requests cost time.** The 6-object requests made stale by a scene switch were still evaluated (about 17 s
  each) before being refused, and the 10-object batch had not run when the session was pulled.
- **The profiler** ran in the same terminal before the batches, so it covered only the load and idle time.

**Fixed after r018:**

- **Batches are paced** (the new default): one request, wait for its answer, print it, then the next. `--all-at-once`
  keeps the old behaviour.
- **Waiting requests whose scene changed** are refused before evaluation (`scene_changed_before_evaluation`).
- **End session** answers every waiting request (`session_ended`) and lets the running one finish before the model
  unloads, so every accepted request is answered.
- **Every result names its snapshot,** and the report groups requests with no known scene as `unknown scene`.
- **The profiler runs in the background** of the same terminal.

The measurement is repeated as the next run.
