# A2.5 delivery 5: the measurement run

Status: **tooling built and tested here; the measurement run is next.** The results and A2.5's closing statuses follow
in sections 4 and 5.

## 1. Scope

The scope comes from D98 ("measured resources") and D99(3) and (7). The choices are mine, for ChatGPT's review.

**One operational session on the headset,** on path U only (cache off, prefix reuse refused):

- every golden dataset command of the 3-, 6- and 10-object snapshots through the ADB inbox (26 in all), with the scene
  switched on the panel between snapshots;
- presets on the first scene;
- the two behaviours delivery 4 has not yet shown on the headset: cancel while queued, and a stale result after a scene
  switch.

**Measured, per D99(3):** each request's stages on the headset clock (detected, validated, queued, inference start and
end, outcome) and the app-observed latency. That is detection to outcome for the inbox, and button event to outcome for
presets. The pipeline's own stage times (build, tokenize, evaluate, score) and the tokens are measured too.

**Memory, as the runtime reports it (D99(7)):**

- llama.cpp's own buffer lines in the session's load log: the model, its repacked copy, the KV cache and the compute
  buffer;
- the wrapper's resident and peak memory (`se_memory_kb`) before and after the load, and after every answered request.

**The PC's push-and-rename times** are reported apart, on the PC clock, and never combined with the headset's.

**Optional: frames and heat,** with A1's existing `tools/profile.py record`, as `docs/profiling.md` describes. It saves
thermal state, battery state and the process list every 30 seconds, and the OVR Metrics CSV: frame rate, stale frames,
CPU and GPU load, app memory.

**Descriptive only:** for golden dataset commands, whether the choice equals the D2 CPU float32 baseline's. No accuracy
claim (D98), and no numerical acceptance.

## 2. The tools

- **Headset:** the delivery-4 session now loads with llama.cpp's load log captured (`startup-log.txt`, verbose during
  the load only). `session.json` records the memory around the load, and every answered request records resident and
  peak memory. `outbox-pull` also brings the load log.
- **`inbox-batch --run RUN --goldens G --snapshot 3|6|10`** sends every golden dataset command of the snapshot, in
  order. The IDs are `m3-01`, `m6-01` and so on.
- **`measure-report --run RUN --goldens G --references REFS`** writes `raw/measure/<UTC time>/summary.json` and
  `report.md`, covering:
  - per scene size and intake: status counts, and app-observed, inference and queue times (median, 90th percentile,
    max);
  - stage times and tokens;
  - memory;
  - the PC's push times;
  - the descriptive agreement.
- **Tests:** `test_quest_measure.py`, 10 checks, worked by hand.
