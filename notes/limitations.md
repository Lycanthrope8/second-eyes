|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2026-09-30 | A1.7d | OVR Metrics writes no rows while the app is frozen, so frame rates count those seconds as frameless (0 fps): an approximation of what the wearer sees | `20260929_A1_r019`, `20260930_A1_r021` | frame-rate results |

| 2026-09-30 | A1.7d | With the model loaded, the headset has 350–600 MB free, and Android pages the idle model out within minutes | `20260929_A1_r018`, `20260929_A1_r019` | memory headroom for speech and the detector |
| 2026-10-04 | A2.2a | Imported IRef-VLA geometry is published annotation in the aligned frame its preprocessing produced: metric by ScanNet's geometry and that path, not measured against the physical room. The inspected VLA-3D code (`a0c023c9662b`) explains the conventions but doesn't certify which producer commit made the released sample | `docs/iref-vla-adapter.md`; each import's `source-manifest.json` | any geometric claim about imported scenes |
| 2026-10-04 | A2.2a | The source's answers assume its 59-object graph: the generator never used the two unknown-category objects as targets, anchors or distractors. In the full 61-object inventory our resolver treats them as possible members of every class, so source answers and our outcomes can differ by inventory view, not only by grounding error | each import's `inventory-views.json`; the A2.2 source review | comparisons with the source's answers |
| 2026-10-04 | A2.2a | The one imported scene (scene0010_01) is development material, inspected in detail. Neither it nor another scan of its room may be presented as held-out evaluation data | A2.2a brief, section 1 | evaluation splits and claims |
| 2026-10-05 | A2.2b | The rules parser is sample-specific: its grammar, 25 relation aliases and ten-colour palette were declared for this one room's templated language. It is not a general English parser, and its coverage of other text is unknown | `docs/iref-vla-evaluation.md` | any claim about language coverage |
| 2026-10-05 | A2.2b | The development measurements come from one inspected room with annotated geometry under provisional relation semantics. Agreement with source labels doesn't show that our relation semantics match the dataset's, and one room offers no independent trials for confidence intervals | the A2.2b report | evaluation claims |
| 2026-10-05 | A2.2b | Resolver timings in the predictions are laptop wall-clock times, dominated by the resolver's per-call validation. They say nothing about headset latency | each prediction's `resolution.diagnostics.timings_s` | latency claims |
| 2026-10-06 | A2.2a, A2.2d | A2.2a's conversion can differ across platforms in the last bit of a computed quaternion component (three values, about 1.1e-16, between a Linux and a Windows conversion), which changes the canonical scene hash, every derived ID and those digits in prompts | ChatGPT's review of the Windows A2.2d evidence (A2.3a brief); `notes/phases/A2.2d_model_inputs.md` addendum | reproducibility claims: a bundle is an artifact to copy, not to regenerate |
| 2026-10-06 | A2.3a | The zero-shot pilot's 32 parents come from parser-conditioned, category-complete subscenes of one inspected room with oracle geometry; there are no viewpoint-dependent commands, its choices are not scored, and its alias order (A..J by object ID, K for ASK) is fixed | `notes/phases/A2.3a_zero_shot_pilot.md` | any reading of the pilot's choices |
| 2026-10-06 | A2.3b | Source agreement counts one annotated target per command: ASK and every non-resolved rules outcome count as not selecting it, so the metric cannot credit a justified abstention, and the rules baseline's unknown-category policy and relation semantics are ours, not the dataset's | `notes/phases/A2.3b_pilot_scoring.md` | any reading of A2.3b's agreement figures |
| 2026-10-07 | A2.3c | The scene document, its object order and its IDs are identical in every cell, so the grid separates the answer letter and the choices-list position only: following one object across the grid shows invariance to those two transformations, not correctness, and may reflect the object's fixed place or ID | D94 design | interpretation of A2.3c |
| 2026-10-07 | A2.3c | The 4,536 cells come from 32 parents of one inspected room: rates are computed per command and averaged over 32 commands per view and format, not treated as independent examples; no significance test or generalization interval | D94 | A2.3c figures |
| 2026-10-07 | A2.3d | 256 parents of the same inspected room, parser-conditioned and category-complete: a broader development screen, not an unseen-room evaluation; the source-known view is an annotation-derived diagnostic condition, not a deployment inventory | D95 | A2.3d figures |
| 2026-10-07 | A2.3d | Correct means agreement with the published target; an ASK on a uniquely annotated command counts as not correct and no ambiguity credit is given; one frozen letter assignment and list order per parent and view, so letter sensitivity remains in every figure | D95 | interpretation of A2.3d |
